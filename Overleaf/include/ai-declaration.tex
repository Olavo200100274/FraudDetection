\chapter*{Declaration on the Use of Generative Artificial Intelligence}
\markboth{Declaration on the Use of Generative Artificial Intelligence}{}

Generative artificial intelligence tools were used as assistive technologies during the development and preparation of this dissertation. Google NotebookLM supported the search for and preliminary exploration of academic literature. Anthropic Claude Code and OpenAI Codex assisted with the development, review, debugging, refactoring, and documentation of analysis code; the organisation and review of experimental workflows; and the verification of traceability and internal consistency between saved experimental artefacts and the numerical values, tables, figures, and interpretations reported in the dissertation. These tools also supported the revision of academic English and cross-chapter coherence, LaTeX editing, and the design and refinement of selected TikZ diagrams and aspects of the document layout.

These tools were not treated as scholarly sources, and their outputs were not accepted as scientific evidence. AI assistance did not replace the execution of experiments or the computation of reported metrics. The results presented in this dissertation originate from the saved outputs of the implemented experimental pipeline. Relevant claims, bibliographic references, and interpretations were checked against the original publications or the corresponding project artefacts before inclusion.

All AI-assisted contributions retained in the dissertation or its supporting project, including code, text, analytical suggestions, and visual material, were critically reviewed and revised where necessary by the author. Final decisions concerning the research objectives, methodology, implementation, selection and interpretation of results, and presentation of the work remained with the author, who assumes full responsibility for the accuracy, originality, and academic integrity of this dissertation.
