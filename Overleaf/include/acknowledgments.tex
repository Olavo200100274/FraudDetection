\chapter*{Acknowledgments}
\markboth{Acknowledgments}{}

The completion of this dissertation would not have been possible without the support and encouragement of many people, to whom I owe a sincere debt of gratitude.

First and foremost, I wish to thank my supervisors, Professor Maryam Abbasi and Professor Pedro Martins, without whose guidance this work would not exist in its present form. This dissertation originated from a proposal put forward by Professor Maryam Abbasi, and it grew steadily into the study it is today through their sustained involvement. I am grateful for their consistent availability, for the constructive feedback provided at every stage of the research process, and for taking the initiative to share orientation materials and resources that proved instrumental in shaping both the methodology and the written work. Their experience in guiding research projects of this nature was evident throughout, and their mentorship was essential to reaching this outcome.

I owe a great deal to my friends and colleagues Vasco Geada and Ivo Camacho, who have accompanied me across every stage of my academic journey at the Instituto Politécnico de Santarém since 2021. From the professional course in Information Systems Programming (TSPSI), through the bachelor's degree in Informatics (LIB1), to the concurrent pursuit of the bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering (LEI) and, finally, this Master's in Applied Informatics (MIA) --- their companionship, motivation, and camaraderie have been a constant throughout. The path through four successive programmes is not an easy one, and having people alongside who share the same commitment makes all the difference. I can say with full conviction that their presence shaped my academic ambitions in ways I did not anticipate at the outset.

I owe a particular debt of gratitude to Professor Filipe Madeira and Professor Filipe Cardoso, who over the years generously shared their professional experience and offered encouragement that I carry with me into my working life. Beyond that continuous support, I wish to acknowledge a specific moment that changed the course of my studies entirely. Having not enrolled in the first admission phase of this master's programme --- genuinely uncertain about whether to continue --- they sought me out during a class, sat with me, and took the time to explain clearly what I stood to lose. That conversation was the turning point. I enrolled in the second phase that same day. For that intervention, and for the path it made possible, I am profoundly grateful.
