\markboth{Acknowledgements}{}

The completion of this dissertation would not have been possible without the support and encouragement of many people, to whom I owe a sincere debt of gratitude.

First and foremost, I wish to thank my supervisors, Professor Maryam Abbasi and Professor Pedro Martins, without whose guidance this work would not exist in its present form. This dissertation originated from a proposal put forward by Professor Maryam Abbasi and developed into its present form through their sustained involvement. I am grateful for their consistent availability, the constructive feedback they provided at every stage of the research process, and their initiative in sharing guidance materials and resources that helped shape both the methodology and the written work. Their experience in supervising research projects of this nature was evident throughout, and their mentorship was essential to bringing this dissertation to completion.

I owe a great deal to my friends and colleagues Vasco Geada and Ivo Camacho, who have accompanied me across every stage of my academic journey at the Instituto Politécnico de Santarém since 2021. From the professional course in Information Systems Programming (TSPSI), through the bachelor's degree in Informatics (LIB1), to the concurrent pursuit of the bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering (LEI) and, finally, this Master's in Applied Informatics (MIA) --- their companionship, motivation, and camaraderie have been a constant throughout. The path through four successive programmes is not an easy one, and having people alongside who share the same commitment makes all the difference. I can say with full conviction that their presence shaped my academic ambitions in ways I did not anticipate at the outset.

I owe a particular debt of gratitude to Professor Filipe Madeira and Professor Filipe Cardoso, who have generously shared their professional experience over the years and offered encouragement that I will carry into my working life. Beyond that continued support, I wish to acknowledge a specific moment that changed the course of my studies. I had not enrolled in the first admission round because I was genuinely uncertain about whether to continue. They sought me out during a class, sat with me, and took the time to explain clearly what I stood to lose. That conversation was the turning point: I enrolled in the second round that same day. For that intervention, and for the path it made possible, I am profoundly grateful.
